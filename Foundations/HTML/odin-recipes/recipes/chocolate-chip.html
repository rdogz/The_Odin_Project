Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

Tasty Chocolate Chip Cookies! Yay!!!

Ingredients

  • 200g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 100g butter
  • 50g white sugar
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C. Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the dry ingredients.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, eggs, vanilla and sugars.
  4. Gradually stir the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough per cookie onto the prepared baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
  7. Bake at 180°C for 9–11 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and the centers still look slightly soft.
  8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
