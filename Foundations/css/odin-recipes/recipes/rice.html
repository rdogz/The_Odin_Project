White Rice

Description

Plain, white rice.

Ingredients

  • 200g of white rice
  • 400ml of water
  • salt

Steps

  1. Wash the rice until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Put the rice on a pan and add the 400ml of water. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
  3. Place the lid on the pan and place the pan over high heat until it boils.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and cook it for 10 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat, let the rice rest, covered, for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve the rice.
