Moscow Mule

Description

My favorite drink, Moscow Mule!

Ingredients

  • 60ml vodka
  • 15ml lime juice
  • 120ml ginger beer
  • Ice cubes
  • Opitional: Lime and Mint decoration

Steps

  1. Fill a copper mug with as much ice as you'd like.
  2. Pour in the vodka and fresh lime juice.
  3. Top with the chilled ginger beer.
  4. Gently stir to combine
  5. Garnish with a lime wedge and, if desired, a sprig of fresh mint.
